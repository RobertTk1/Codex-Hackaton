# Chat Onboarding — Account Turn Interaction Specification

Screen ID: `27d64888-57dc-4ecd-a4b2-7bbaecc5ef0f`

The compact authentication component appears beneath `Where should I send your style report?` Google and email magic link are the only methods. Existing credentials sign in; new credentials create the account; both connect the anonymous conversation and resume in place. Link-sent, retry, and change-email messages stay in this turn. The component never adds passwords, tabs, marketing opt-ins, or a separate account page.

Shared behavior: `shared-contract.md`.
