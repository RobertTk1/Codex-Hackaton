# Chat Onboarding — Photo Recovery Interaction Specification

Screen ID: `0903cde1-37cb-49c1-8146-ecd07dd54c70`

The inline gallery shows only enough information to identify each upload and its status. `Try again` retries the failed photo only; `Remove` removes that photo with undo. `Drag to reorder` has keyboard move and accessible position-menu equivalents and commits the exact current photo order atomically. Eight Ready photos unlock `Keep going` even if optional extras are checking or failed. The component collapses to a short factual confirmation after continuation.

Shared behavior: `shared-contract.md`.
