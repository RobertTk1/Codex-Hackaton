# Chat Onboarding — Visual Taste Turn Interaction Specification

Screen ID: `fd161337-14fc-4476-9cec-9d004248c1c7`

One visual card appears directly beneath `How do you feel about this look?` Right/right arrow/`Love`, left/left arrow/`Hate`, and down/down arrow/`Maybe` are equivalent. After selection, the response appears as a one-word customer message, the current card collapses, and the next visual turn arrives. `Undo` reopens the prior card. A loading failure keeps the choice count and offers Retry.

Shared behavior: `shared-contract.md`.
