## Desktop generation brief

Create one flat, straight-on, very tall full-page desktop frontend raster comp. No browser chrome, device frame, presentation board, annotations, or alternate states. Preserve the approved 1440 px desktop wireframe composition and all nine sections.

Art direction: Acid Dispatch / Editorial Edge. Pristine White and Cloud paper-like fields grounded by Ink, with Acid reserved for primary actions and sharp editorial signals. Use the supplied Magic Mirror logo faithfully. Pair dramatic Instrument Serif display moments with Space Grotesk UI clarity. The hero is image-led and cinematic but codeable: an art-directed full-body fashion image or tightly controlled editorial look-set that makes the product promise tangible. The report preview should look like a believable premium style report, not a generic dashboard. Use off-grid editorial tension, layered image crops, precise vertical rules, generous whitespace, and restrained 24 px cards.

Keep copy visually secondary to product imagery and interface. One obvious primary action per decision point. Maintain accessible contrast, plausible controls, consistent 999 px buttons, and strong implementation clarity. Avoid AI glows, glassmorphism, blobs, fake proof, distorted bodies, over-packed sections, or repeated card grids.

## Mobile generation brief

Create one flat, straight-on, very tall full-page mobile frontend raster comp. No phone frame, browser chrome, presentation board, annotations, or alternate states. Preserve the approved 390 px mobile wireframe as its own composition: single narrative column, intentional fashion-image recrops, full-width primary actions, generous touch targets, and all nine sections in their approved order.

Use the same approved desktop visual system without shrinking desktop. Keep the logo readable, hide desktop-only navigation behind a compact menu treatment if the wireframe calls for it, keep the hero decisive, and ensure report modules and FAQ rows remain legible. Preserve the same White, Cloud, Ink, Acid, Pale, and Deep roles; Instrument Serif editorial moments; Space Grotesk product copy; rounded actions; and precise focus-bracket details.

## QA target

The output must read immediately as a real Magic Mirror landing page, not a moodboard. It must preserve the approved journey, show a tangible personalized style-report experience, remain feasible to build, and contain no implied backend capability or proof beyond the approved product contract.
