# Information Architecture

Status: Approved by Talisha White on 2026-07-19  
Source: Approved PRD, user stories, scenarios, and screen inventory

## Public

- Landing page
- Unified account access

## Anonymous style-report onboarding

- Personal profile
- Brand and category sizes
- Outfit-photo consent and upload
- Taste calibration
- Permanent account connection
- Report analysis

## Authenticated report and styling

- Style home
- Report overview
  - Color
  - Body-style guidance
  - Recommendations
  - Feedback and recalibration
- Live styling
  - Camera permission and recovery
  - Connection and visualization states
  - Voice permission and interaction states
  - Hand-gesture guide and recognition states
  - Product details
- Bag
- Retailer handoff

## System boundaries

- Anonymous identity owns onboarding progress until permanent account connection.
- Google and email magic link share one signup/login entry.
- Retailers own current price, stock, checkout, payment, shipping, and returns.
- Camera, microphone, and photo-analysis consent are purpose-specific.
