# Flow 04 — Bag and Retailer Handoff

Related scenario: SC-012

1. Customer opens a recommended product from the report or live session.
2. Product detail shows retailer provenance, current known price/availability, size context, and why the item fits the report.
3. Customer adds the item to the Magic Mirror bag without leaving the current session.
4. Bag groups products by retailer and refreshes availability before handoff.
5. Customer chooses **Continue to retailer** for a retailer group.
6. Magic Mirror confirms that the retailer owns price, stock, checkout, payment, fulfillment, returns, and terms.
7. Customer continues to the correct retailer destination or stays in Magic Mirror.

Completion signal: the customer reaches the correct retailer with product context while their Magic Mirror report, session, and bag remain intact for return.
