Include:

1. Brand summary.
2. Source assets.
3. Logo system.
4. Logo usage rules.
5. What to avoid.
6. Primary palette.
7. Secondary shades/tints.
8. WCAG-approved pairings.
9. Buttons and links.
10. Badges, callouts, headers, tooltips.
11. Typography system.
12. Typography avoid rules.
13. Application examples and mockup paths.
14. Production notes and caveats.

Use the positioning sentence from `GOAL.md`, the exact assets under `company/brand/exports/`, the real selected font sources, and the Acid Dispatch anchors. Explicitly distinguish the standard wordmark, the combined lockup with the icon replacing the `o`, the complete standalone icon, and the simplified 16px favicon.

`DESIGN.md` should match the final PDF and token files. Resolve local links before marking complete.
